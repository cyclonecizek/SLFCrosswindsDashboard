Guidance sources

    Same models, members and weights as the 7-Day board. Sources you switch off here stay off on this page only.

    Components. The angle is the wind direction minus the runway heading (true). Crosswind is wind × |sin(angle)|; the along-runway part is wind × cos(angle), a headwind when positive and a tailwind when negative. Either end checks both runway ends at once, the way the 7-Day board does: any wind along the runway is a headwind on one end and a tailwind on the other, so the headwind and tailwind limits are compared with its size.

    Observations. Towers 0511, 0512 and 0513 line the runway and measure at 30 ft. Peak / gust uses the one-minute peak with its direction; Sustained uses the one-minute mean. KTTS uses the reported gust when there is one, otherwise the sustained wind. A variable wind is treated as fully across and along the runway. Last hours draws every one-minute tower reading kept by the runway winds service (it stores 24 hours) with each KTTS report as a square; without the service it shows what this page has collected since it opened.

    Forecast. Every member of every source on the 7-Day board is judged each hour against your limits, using its 10 m gust (or sustained wind) and direction. A source’s chance is the share of its members over a limit; the blend weights sources the same way the board does. For a block or window, a member counts once if any hour it has in it breaks a limit, and it has to cover at least half the window’s hours. Hi-res models reach about 2 days; after that the global ensembles and blends carry the forecast. Values are rounded to the nearest 5%.
