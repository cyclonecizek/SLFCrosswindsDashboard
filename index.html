Components use wind direction and runway headings in degrees true. Crosswind is wind speed × |sin(angle)|. The along-runway component is a headwind when positive and a tailwind when negative. Either end compares the magnitude against both enabled along-runway limits.

Towers 0511, 0512, and 0513 use the exact 30 ft level. Peak / gust uses the one-minute peak and its direction. If peak direction is missing, the component uses the mean direction and is marked approximate. KTTS uses its reported gust when available. Unknown wind direction is treated as fully across and along the runway.

The 10-min highest option shows the largest component observed in the preceding 10 minutes. It is calculated from the available one-minute records. Missing periods remain gaps. Limits are display settings saved in this browser.
